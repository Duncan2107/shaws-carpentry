About Shaws Carpentry

Built on Care, Finished With Precision

At Shaws Carpentry, every project starts with listening. Whether it's a fitted kitchen, wardrobes that make the most of an awkward alcove, or a new garden deck, we take the time to understand your space and build carpentry that belongs in it.

We treat every home as if it were our own. We turn up when we say we will, keep the workspace tidy, and never cut corners on materials or finish.

We take on domestic and commercial work alike, covering Sussex and Kent for homes, and Sussex, Kent and London for commercial projects.

  • Bespoke designs, made to measure
  • Quality timber and hardware, honestly priced
  • Clean, considerate and reliable on site
  • Free, no-obligation quotes
